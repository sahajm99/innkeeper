About this demo

Innkeeper is a rebuild of a university database course project: a hotel management system for a small Texas chain. Everything in it is fictional and it is wiped and re-seeded every night.

The course version was a Swing desktop application over MySQL, and it could double-book a room, because it checked availability by room type rather than by room. This one holds one row per room and night behind a unique index, so the database refuses the second booking however many people press the button at once. The button on the front page proves it ten at a time.

The schema is not the one the course handed out. Every change and the reason for it is listed in SCHEMA-CHANGES.
